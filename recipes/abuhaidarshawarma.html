Abu Haidar Inspired Shawarma

Description

A meat shawarma recipe from the Middle Eats youtube channel in a similar style to the famous Abu Haidar restuarant.

Ingredients

Meat Shawarma

  • 1kg of Beef or Lamb. Use a flavourful cut with a good amount of fat. Stewing meat works well but Ribeye, Chuck, or Beef ribs are best
  • 50-100g Lamb fat or dry aged beef fat (optional, but highly recommended. Double if using lean meat) OR 3 Tbsp of Beef drippings or duck fat (in place of the lamb fat)
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Green Chilli (your preferred heat level)
  • 12 Soft flour buns

Marinade

  • 2 Medium Onions
  • Zest of 1 Lemon
  • Zest of 1 Orange
  • 5 Tbsp White Vinegar
  • 2 Tsp Salt
  • 1 Tsp Pepper
  • 1/2 Tsp Sumac
  • 1/2 Tsp Baharat (leave it out if you don't have it)

Tahini Sauce

  • 150g Yoghurt
  • 100g Tahina
  • 100g Water
  • 40g Lemon Juice
  • 1/2 Tsp Salt
  • 1/2 Tsp Pepper
  • 1/2 Tsp Cumin
  • 1/2 Tsp Chilli Powder (Optional)

Onion and Parsley Salads

  • 1 Red Onion
  • 25g or small bunch of parsley
  • 1/2 Tsp Salt
  • 1 Tsp Sumac

Steps

  1. Leave meat in freezer for about an hour to firm up.
  2. Slice meat into steaks about 1cm thick, then stack on top of each other and slice on the short side to make them into strips.
  3. Grate an onion OR roughly chop it and grind it down to a pulp in a food processor.
  4. Zest an orange and a lemon with a microplane and add it to the onion pulp. Add the salt, black pepper, sumac, and Baharat (if you have it). Add white vinegar and then blend the marinade.
  5. Add the marinade to a bowl with the meat and mix thoroughly. Let sit for as long as you can to fully marinade (3-4 hours or even overnight).
  6. After marinading, add the whole mixture to a slow cooker or a large pot and level it out. Nestle in the green chiles and the tomato.
  7. Put the pot on high heat and bring it up to a steamy boil the turn it down to low letting the meat cook low and slow until tender. (OPTIONAL - add the additional fat at this point)
  8. Put a lid on the pot, turn the heat to medium-low, and allow the meat to cook from 30-180 minutes depending on the cut of the meat. Periodically check with a fork to see if the meat is tender. Be sure the pot has liquid so the meat doesn't burn.
  9. Make the tahini sauce by combining tahini, yogurt, water, and freshly squeezed lemon juice. Season with salt, black pepper, cumin, and chile powder and combine.
  10. Make the onion and parsley salad by combining red onion cut into strips with salt and sumac in a bowl. Massage the mixture by hand to encourage the onions to wilt slightly and let sit for about 15 minutes. Chop up some parsley and add to the mixture at the end.
  11. Return to the pot and remove the lid, allow for around 15 minutes for the liquid to cook off the meat mixture and allow for some browning.
  12. Cut the heat and allow for the mixture to cool before adding half the tahini sauce and the masserated onions and chopped parsely. Mix to combine
  13. Assemble the sandwiches and enjoy!
